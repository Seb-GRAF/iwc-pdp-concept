    About the collection

    90 years of Pilot’s Watches

    Since the first Special Pilot’s Watch of 1936, the brief has stayed the same: a dial you can read at a glance, in any light, on a watch built for the cockpit.

    Discover the collection

    Since 1868

    Made in Schaffhausen

    The American watchmaker Florentine Ariosto Jones founded IWC in Schaffhausen in 1868, combining the best craftsmanship and technology. Every IWC watch is still assembled by trained watchmakers, then inspected and tested.
